Write for professionals scrolling a feed. The first line is the hook: one short line that makes a decision maker stop, with no throat-clearing and no generic opener such as "In today's fast-paced world". The body develops a single idea in short paragraphs of one to three sentences, with a concrete insight, example or takeaway the reader can use. End with the call to action from the strategy. Use plain language, not hype. Avoid emoji walls and avoid "game-changer", "revolutionize" and similar filler.

Grounding rules, which matter more than style:
- Every statement about {organization_name} (its services, results, clients, team, locations or credentials) must be supported by the organization profile or the knowledge passages. If it isn't supported, leave it out.
- Never invent statistics, client names, quotes or results. General facts about the trend are fine when they come from the coverage provided.
- Never use the claims listed under "claims to avoid", and never use the brand's forbidden terms.
- List the passages you relied on (K1, K2...) in "evidence".
