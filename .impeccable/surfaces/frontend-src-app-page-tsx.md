# ContentPulse: landing page and app (one world)

Scope: the public landing page at "/" (Persuade) and the signed-in app (Operate) share one visual world. Audience: marketing agencies running content for several clients. Job on the landing page: understand "trends that fit each client, carried to approved posts" and start (Start free / Sign in). Job in the app: move a post from trend to "Ready to publish" fast. Proof available: the product itself only; demonstration feed items are labelled "Example". Constraints: keep coloured status tags with words (user request), Operate clarity first, WCAG 2.2 AA, light and dark.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A newsroom wire desk. Trends arrive as wire copy; the desk spikes what doesn't fit the client and runs what does. Refuses the category default: gradient hero, floating dashboard screenshot, three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm-grey newsprint ground on an eleven-step paper-to-ink ramp (the only neutrals), black ink, FLASH red reserved for "fits / urgent / primary run", wire blue for links, selection and focus. Slug lines, datelines, timestamps and scores in a teleprinter mono; headlines in a condensed news grotesk; UI text in a plain sans. Hairline rules, perforated tape edges, priority flags (FLASH, URGENT, ROUTINE) as words, a spike for rejects, an OK stamp for approved.

STORY: The visitor sees a live wire of real-looking trend copy, watches the desk flag the items that fit an example client and set one as a finished post, understands that ContentPulse filters by fit and carries stories to approval, and starts a free account.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black masthead strip: wordmark left, live clock and "Sign in" right. Left 40%: a perforated wire tape scrolling upward, items as slug + dateline + one-line copy + fit score, URGENT flags in red on items that fit. Right 60%: headline "Read the wire. Run what fits." at 5-6rem condensed, one line of explanation, Start free (ink button) and Sign in, then the desk card: the flagged item set as a LinkedIn post with an OK stamp.

FORM: The Wire Desk, my top-ranked candidate (1 of 7), chosen by the user as IMPECCABLE'S PICK; seed key 17c02930. Raises kept: one 0-100 fit scale everywhere; neutrals from one tonal ramp; every status a named mark with a word; each trend leads with the post it could become. Signature interaction: the live wire, where a fitting item gets flagged URGENT and is set on the desk as a post. Motion grammar: teleprinter-paced upward feed, short ease-out, paused on hover and under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
